Dr. Aria Rastegar

Researcher in Linguistics based in Nuremberg, Germany

About

I investigate how language is understood. Do we process language word-by-word, or do we draw on larger prefabricated chunks and constructions? How do compositional and non-compositional processes interact as we comprehend language? What role do meaning, function, and pragmatic context play in this process? To answer questions like these, I use experimental methods, computational approaches, and statistical analysis.

My PhD work focuses on cross-linguistic comprehension of idiomatic constructions in L1 and L2 speakers of Persian, German, and English. I believe this cross-linguistic approach can address many other fundamental questions in language understanding.
