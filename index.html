About Me

I investigate how language is understood[cite: 4]. Do we process language word-by-word, or do we draw on larger prefabricated chunks and constructions[cite: 4]? How do compositional and non-compositional processes interact as we comprehend language[cite: 5]? What role do meaning, function, and pragmatic context play in this process[cite: 6]? To answer questions like these, I use experimental methods, computational approaches, and statistical analysis[cite: 7].

My PhD work focuses on cross-linguistic comprehension of idiomatic constructions in L1 and L2 speakers of Persian, German, and English[cite: 8]. I believe this cross-linguistic approach can address many other fundamental questions in language understanding[cite: 9].
